https://opentelemetry.io/docs/zero-code/java/agent/getting-started/

In the VM Options of IntelliJ
    -javaagent:opentelemetry-javaagent.jar
    -Dotel.service.name=sample-service
    -Dotel.exporter.otlp.endpoint=http://localhost:4317
    -Dotel.logs.exporter=otlp
    -Dotel.traces.exporter=none
    -Dotel.metrics.exporter=otlp
    -Dotel.exporter.otlp.protocol=grpc

Open grafana: localhost:3000
    Data Sources > Add Loki > url: http://loki:3100 > Test and Save
    Explore > Logs
    Search {service_name="sample-service"}
    
GET http://localhost:8080/api/v1/sample
GET http://localhost:8080/api/v1/sample/error-demo
